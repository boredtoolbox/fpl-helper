Log in at fantasy.premierleague.com and open Points. The address bar reads /entry/1234567/event/… — that number is your team id. Pasting the whole URL works too. More than one team? Separate them with commas.

Only used to read what FPL creators are saying on YouTube and in the press. Leave it blank and everything else still works — the projections are pure maths and do not involve it at all. Get your own free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. It must be yours; keys are per-person and come with their own quota.

The first fetch takes 10-15 minutes, nearly all of it walking the FPL API at one request per second. After that it refreshes itself once a day and takes seconds.
